# Japan Job Loss Check

Free educational checklist for quitting or losing a job in Japan: ISA 14-day organisation notice, ~3-month inactivity risk, Hello Work / 雇用保険 eligibility and waiting rules (including the April 2025 self-quit change), health insurance (任意継続 vs 国保), National Pension switch, and documents.

- Site: https://japan-job-loss.vercel.app
- API: `GET/POST /api/check`, `GET /api/rules` (keyless, CORS `*`)
- Agent docs: `/llms.txt`, `/openapi.json`, `/.well-known/ai-plugin.json`
- MCP: `japan_job_loss_check` on https://free-agent-tools.vercel.app/mcp

Not immigration, labour or benefits advice. Not affiliated with ISA, MHLW, Hello Work, the Japan Pension Service, 協会けんぽ or any city office. Rules as of 2026-11-07.
